WHAT TO

Which truck details help most?

Stock number, preferred make/model, budget, mileage limit and required transmission or sleeper configuration.

What should I include for a trade-in?

Year, make, model, current mileage, major equipment, current condition and any available service/history information.

Are the current listings live inventory?

No. The included inventory is explicitly demonstration data until the dealership replaces it with verified listings.
